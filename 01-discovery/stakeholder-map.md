| Stakeholder | Role | Entity / Scope | What they care about | Influence on this project |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sarah Whitfield | Group CFO | All entities | Close speed, board-ready reporting, control risk | Buyer / final sign-off |

| External Auditor (annual) | Audit firm | Group | Control evidence, audit trail, segregation of duties | Indirect — shapes control requirements |

## Notes on engagement approach
- CFO and Group Controller are the primary discovery contacts — they set priorities and sponsor the initiative.
- Entity-level controllers (UK/US) surface currency, statutory, and local-process nuance.
- Shared Services staff surface the real day-to-day manual pain (most valuable detail for requirements).
- IT is engaged early to sanity-check technical feasibility, not as an afterthought.
- Auditor perspective is used indirectly (via the Controller) to shape control requirements — no direct interview assumed.
